Understanding Response Sheet vs Answer Key in JEE Advanced

Candidate Response Sheet (ORS)

  • Contains the exact options chosen by the candidate during the computer-based test (CBT).
  • Released separately for Paper 1 and Paper 2 inside the Candidate Portal.
  • Displays Question Code, chosen options, and marked status.
  • Essential for calculating accurate raw marks before results.

Official Answer Key (Provisional & Final)

  • Formulated and verified by IIT professors across Physics, Chemistry, and Math.
  • Published as public PDF documents accessible to all candidates.
  • Includes valid numerical ranges (e.g. 3.45 to 3.55) and multi-correct combinations.
  • Final key integrates dropped questions and revised multi-keys.

How to Submit Feedback / Objections on JEE Advanced Key

The conducting IIT provides an online feedback mechanism on the Candidate Portal where aspirants can point out ambiguities or challenge provisional keys:

1. Log In to Feedback Portal

Access the feedback section inside your candidate dashboard on jeeadv.ac.in during the 48-hour active window.

2. Select Paper & Question

Choose Paper 1 or Paper 2, select the subject (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics), and pick the specific Question ID.

3. Enter Comments & Justifications

Provide clear mathematical/scientific justification or cite standard academic textbooks supporting your alternative answer.

4. Expert Committee Review

The Joint Implementation Committee (JIC) reviews all candidate feedback with domain professors before sealing the Final Key.

Universal Revision: When IIT accepts an objection or drops an ambiguous question, the modification is applied uniformly to all candidates. No individual email confirmations are sent to candidates who submitted feedback.

Marking Scheme & Partial Marking Calculation Guide

Unlike JEE Main, JEE Advanced features dynamic marking schemes including partial marking in Multiple Choice Questions (one or more than one correct option):

Question Type Condition / Candidate Selection Marks Awarded
Multiple Correct Options (MCQs)
(Full Marks: +4, Negative: -2)		 All correct options are chosen (e.g. A, B, D when A, B, D are correct) +4 Marks
All 4 options are correct, but candidate marks only 3 correct options +3 Marks
3 or more options correct, but candidate marks only 2 correct options +2 Marks
2 or more options correct, but candidate marks only 1 correct option +1 Mark
Candidate selects ANY incorrect option (even if correct options are also selected) -2 Marks
Single Correct Option (MCQs) Correct option chosen / Incorrect option chosen +3 Marks / -1 Mark
Non-Negative Numerical Value (NAT) Correct numerical value / Incorrect or Unattempted +3 or +4 Marks / 0 Marks (No negative marking)
Matrix Match / List Match All matches correct / Any incorrect match +3 Marks / -1 Mark
JEE Advanced Aggregate Score Formula
Total Aggregate Score = (Paper 1 Physics + Chem + Math) + (Paper 2 Physics + Chem + Math)

Total Marks across Paper 1 and Paper 2 usually range between 360 to 396 marks depending on the question paper design set by the conducting IIT.
