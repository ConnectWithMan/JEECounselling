JEE Advanced 2027 Admit Card Release Schedule & Highlights

The JEE Advanced Admit Card is organized and released by the 7 Zonal IITs exclusively for candidates who rank among the top 2,50,000 performers in JEE Main and successfully complete the JEE Advanced registration process. It serves as an official entry ticket for both Paper 1 and Paper 2. Candidates must review the complete JEE Advanced exam dates to stay updated on the entire schedule.

Official Release Window: The JEE Advanced hall ticket will be accessible for download online starting from Sunday, May 11, 2027 (10:00 IST) through Sunday, May 17, 2027 (14:30 IST) on the official website jeeadv.ac.in.

How to Download Your JEE Advanced Hall Ticket 2027

To obtain your JEE Advanced hall ticket, follow the simple steps outlined below via the candidate portal:

1

Step 1: Access Official Portal

Navigate to the official portal at jeeadv.ac.in. Locate and click on the link "JEE Advanced 2027 Admit Card Download Link" or open the Candidate Portal directly.

2

Step 2: Enter Candidate Login Credentials

Enter your JEE Advanced Registration Number (or JEE Main Application Number), Date of Birth (DD/MM/YYYY), Password, and Mobile Number as requested on screen.

3

Step 3: Verify Captcha & Submit

Complete the security captcha/pin challenge and click on the 'Submit / Login' button.

4

Step 4: Download & Print PDF

Your JEE Advanced admit card will display. Download the document in PDF format and take clear printouts on A4 paper for test center verification.

Physical Verification Notice: The official printed copy of your admit card will be physically stamped and verified during Paper 1 at the examination hall. You must preserve this original stamped admit card carefully until the complete JoSAA seat allocation process is finished.

Common Download Issues & Resolving Errors

During the download window, candidates may occasionally run into issues due to heavy portal traffic or data mismatches. Here is how to handle them:

Common Downloading Issues

  • Incorrect Login Credentials: Re-check your password or date of birth format. Use the 'Forgot Password' or 'Find Registration Number' option if needed.
  • Website Traffic & Server Congestion: High traffic when admit cards go live can slow down the portal. Retry during non-peak hours (early morning or late evening).
  • Admit Card Sent by Post? No. Zonal IITs do NOT dispatch admit cards via postal mail or courier. Online download is mandatory.

Resolving Discrepancies: If you spot any discrepancy such as spelling errors in your name, wrong category, photo mismatch, or incorrect center allocation, immediately contact the Chairman of JEE Advanced at your respective Zonal Coordinating IIT for immediate rectification.

Forgot Registration Number? Recovery & Replacement Guide

If you misplace or forget your JEE Advanced registration number, follow these retrieval steps on the candidate portal:

Recover Registration Number

  1. Visit jeeadv.ac.in and open the Candidate Portal.
  2. Click on the link 'Find Registration Number'.
  3. Provide your JEE Main 2027 Application Number and Date of Birth.
  4. Click 'Submit' to view your forgotten JEE Advanced registration number on screen.

Lost Original Stamped Admit Card?

If you lose your original stamped admit card after Paper 1, immediately contact the Chairman of JEE Advanced at the coordinating Zonal IIT for your region to request an official replacement.

Required Documents & Exam Day Guidelines

Your admission to the exam center is contingent on bringing the mandatory items listed below:

Must-Carry Items at Exam Center

  • Printed Admit Card: Hard copy downloaded from jeeadv.ac.in.
  • Original Photo Identity Card: Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID, PAN Card, Driving License, School/College ID, or Notarized Photo Certificate. (Must be original, valid, and non-expired).
  • Stationery: Black/blue ballpoint pens and pencils for scratch work.
  • Water Bottle: One transparent drinking water bottle.
  • Analogue Watch: Simple analogue wrist watch without any smart connectivity features.
  • Safety Items: Face mask and personal hand sanitizer.

Essential Exam Day Rules

  • Both Papers are Mandatory: Appearing in BOTH Paper 1 (Morning) and Paper 2 (Afternoon) is compulsory. Missing either paper results in automatic disqualification.
  • Punctuality: Arrive early at the designated exam center to complete biometric verification and frisking without delay.
  • No Early Exits: Candidates are strictly not permitted to leave the examination hall until the full duration of the shift concludes.
  • Prohibited Items: Mobile phones, smartwatches, calculators, Bluetooth devices, wallets, metallic jewelry, caps, or paper notes are strictly banned.
