How to Check & Download JEE Advanced 2027 Rank Card

Qualifying candidates can download their digital rank card directly from the official IIT portal by following these steps:

1

Visit Portal

Go to the official portal at jeeadv.ac.in or the result mirror server designated by the organizing IIT.

2

Click Result Link

Click on the headline link "JEE (Advanced) 2027 Result Portal / Candidate Portal".

3

Enter Login Details

Provide your JEE Advanced Roll Number, Date of Birth (DD/MM/YYYY), and registered 10-digit Mobile Number.

4

View & Save Scorecard

Review your total marks, subject marks in Paper 1 and 2, CRL Rank, Category Rank, and print 3+ physical copies for JoSAA.

No Physical Dispatch: Physical rank cards are not dispatched via courier or post. The digital scorecard downloaded from jeeadv.ac.in is the only official document recognized by JoSAA and reporting IIT institutes.

Official Tie-Breaking Resolution in JEE Advanced 2027

When two or more candidates obtain identical aggregate total marks in JEE Advanced, inter-se merit is determined sequentially as follows:

1

Higher Positive Marks

Candidate with higher positive marks in the examination is awarded the higher rank.

2

Mathematics Marks

Candidate scoring higher marks in Mathematics receives the higher rank.

3

Physics Marks

Candidate scoring higher marks in Physics receives the priority ranking.

4

Same Rank Assigned

If the tie is still not resolved, both candidates are awarded the same rank (no candidate age bias).

Architecture Aptitude Test (AAT 2027) for B.Arch at IITs

Candidates aspiring for the 5-year B.Arch program at IIT Roorkee, IIT Kharagpur, and IIT (BHU) Varanasi must qualify in AAT 2027.

AAT Exam Format & Structure

  • Free drawing, geometrical drawing, architectural awareness
  • 3-Hour Pen-and-Paper Offline Test at all 7 Zonal IITs
  • Only "Pass / Fail" status awarded (No separate marks)
  • Admission strictly based on JEE Advanced CRL rank of qualifiers

Key AAT Milestones

  • Online Registration: Opens immediately after Advanced result
  • Exam Date: June 12, 2027 (Single Morning Session)
  • Result Declaration: June 15, 2027 on jeeadv.ac.in
  • JoSAA Choice Filling: Select B.Arch programs before choice locking

Frequently Asked Questions on JEE Advanced Results

Everything you need to know regarding scorecards, minimum subject cutoffs, tie-breakers, and JoSAA seat locking.

What are the minimum qualifying marks required to get a rank in JEE Advanced 2027?

Candidates must achieve both: (1) Minimum percentage of marks in each subject (Physics, Chemistry, Math - typically 10% for General CRL, 9% for OBC/EWS, 5% for SC/ST/PwD), and (2) Minimum aggregate percentage of marks (typically 35% for General CRL, 31.5% for OBC/EWS, 17.5% for SC/ST/PwD).

If I only appear for Paper 1 of JEE Advanced, will my result be evaluated?

No. It is strictly mandatory to sit for both Paper 1 and Paper 2. If a candidate is absent in either paper, their answer scripts are not evaluated, and no rank is awarded.

What is the tie-breaking rule in JEE Advanced 2027 if two candidates have the same aggregate marks?

Ties in aggregate marks are resolved sequentially by: 1. Higher positive marks scored, 2. Higher marks in Mathematics, 3. Higher marks in Physics, 4. Higher marks in Chemistry. If a tie remains unresolved, both candidates are awarded the identical rank.

How can I register for the Architecture Aptitude Test (AAT) after results?

Candidates who qualify in JEE Advanced 2027 can register for AAT 2027 on jeeadv.ac.in immediately after result declaration for admission to B.Arch programs at IIT Roorkee, IIT Kharagpur, and IIT (BHU) Varanasi.

Will physical rank cards be sent by post to my address?

No, IIT conducting authorities do not send physical rank cards by post. You must download the digital scorecard from jeeadv.ac.in and print it for JoSAA verification.
