Overview of JEE Advanced 2027 Counselling

Securing a rank in JEE Advanced qualifies candidates for admission into India's premier 23 Indian Institutes of Technology (IITs). Seat allocation for all IITs is managed through the centralized JoSAA (Joint Seat Allocation Authority) portal.

Unlike the NIT+ system, admission to IITs has distinct regulatory frameworks: there is no state domicile quota (all seats are 100% All India Quota), and IITs never participate in CSAB Special Rounds. If a candidate misses choice locking or forfeits an IIT seat during JoSAA, no subsequent spot round exists to reclaim that seat.

Eligibility Criteria for IIT Seat Allocation

Securing an All India Rank (AIR) in JEE Advanced is the primary requirement, but applicants must also fulfill strict performance and board criteria:

JEE Advanced 2027 Merit Rank

  • Must score above the minimum subject-wise cutoff marks in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics in JEE Advanced 2027.
  • Must secure aggregate marks above the minimum aggregate qualifying cutoff for their category.
  • Must be awarded a rank in the Common Rank List (CRL) or relevant Category Rank List.
  • Candidates can appear for JEE Advanced a maximum of two times in consecutive years.

Class 12 Board Benchmark

  • General, OBC-NCL & EWS: At least 75% aggregate marks in Class 12 board examinations.
  • SC, ST & PwD: At least 65% aggregate marks in Class 12.
  • Top 20 Percentile Alternative: Candidates who feature within the top 20 percentile of their respective board are fully eligible.
  • Five compulsory subjects: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, a language, and any fifth subject.

Step-by-Step JoSAA Counselling Procedure for IITs

Admission into the 23 IITs strictly follows the centralized JoSAA computer allocation process:

1

JoSAA Authentication & Profile Check

Log in at josaa.nic.in with your JEE Main Application Number and JEE Advanced password. The system retrieves your JEE Advanced CRL rank and category status automatically.

2

Strategic Choice Filling for IITs

Browse available branches across all 23 IITs. Candidates can fill 4-Year B.Tech, 4-Year BS, 5-Year Dual Degree (B.Tech + M.Tech), and Integrated M.Tech programs. If qualified in AAT, B.Arch programs at IIT Kharagpur, Roorkee, and BHU become available.

3

Reviewing Mock Allocations & Locking

Two mock allotments give you realistic visibility into what branch and IIT your rank commands. Before the deadline, review your list and permanently lock your choices with your password.

4

Seat Allotment Result & Willingness Submission

When an IIT seat is allocated in any round, submit your online willingness: Freeze (confirm seat, exit rounds), Float (hold seat, upgrade to any higher IIT choice), or Slide (upgrade within the same IIT).

5

Payment of Seat Acceptance Fee (SAF) & Document Upload

Pay ₹35,000 (GEN/OBC-NCL/EWS) or ₹17,500 (SC/ST/PwD) online. Upload scanned copies of Class 10 & 12 certificates, category certificate (issued after April 1, 2027), medical certificate, and cancelled cheque.

6

Online Document Verification & Dual Reporting

Verifying officers at IIT reporting centers review your credentials online. If you previously held an NIT seat and received an IIT seat upgrade in a subsequent round, JoSAA handles dual reporting automatically, but you must complete online verification for the IIT.

7

Physical Reporting & Campus Admission

Following round 5/6, download the final JoSAA Seat Confirmation Letter. Report physically to the allotted IIT campus on the specified orientation date with original certificates to complete admission formalities.

Architecture Aptitude Test (AAT 2027) Admissions

Architecture aspirants wishing to study the 5-year B.Arch program at IITs must qualify for the Architecture Aptitude Test (AAT). AAT is conducted by the organizing IIT at selected zonal IIT campuses shortly after JEE Advanced results.

IITs Offering B.Arch

  • IIT Kharagpur: Department of Architecture & Regional Planning (~45 Seats).
  • IIT Roorkee: Department of Architecture & Planning (~37 Seats).
  • IIT (BHU) Varanasi: Department of Architecture, Planning & Design (~26 Seats).

AAT Test & Allotment Rules

  • Only candidates who qualify in JEE Advanced 2027 are eligible to appear for AAT.
  • No separate ranking is generated for AAT; the result is declared simply as 'Pass' or 'Fail'.
  • Seat allotment in B.Arch is strictly based on the candidate's JEE Advanced CRL rank among AAT-qualified candidates.

Female Supernumerary Quota in 23 IITs

To foster gender diversity across premier engineering programs, the Ministry of Education implemented supernumerary seats targeting a minimum of 20% female enrollment in every academic program across all 23 IITs.

How Female Supernumerary Seats Work

  • These seats are strictly supernumerary (extra); they do not eat into the general neutral pool.
  • Female candidates are first considered for Gender-Neutral seats purely on merit.
  • If the 20% threshold is not naturally met by merit, supernumerary seats are triggered exclusively for females.

Significant Cutoff Advantage

  • Female-only closing ranks are significantly more relaxed compared to gender-neutral cutoffs.
  • For example, IIT Bombay CSE closes at CRL ~68 (Gender-Neutral) vs ~290 (Female-Only).
  • Female aspirants should fill dream branches generously to maximize admission odds.

IIT Choice-Filling Strategy: College vs Branch Dilemma

The dilemma between choosing a top-5 IIT with a non-circuital branch versus choosing a 2nd-generation IIT with Computer Science is a dilemma faced by almost every JEE Advanced qualifier:

Top-5 IIT Core Branch Advantage

  • Unmatched alumni networks, venture capital access, and startup culture in Bombay, Delhi, Madras.
  • Consulting and financial firms (McKinsey, BCG, Goldman Sachs) open hiring to all branches.
  • Option to pursue a minor in Computer Science or Data Science alongside core engineering.

Gen-2/3 IIT CSE Advantage

  • Direct placement eligibility in high-paying product tech companies (Google, Microsoft, Uber).
  • IIT Hyderabad and IIT Indore have state-of-the-art research labs and stellar median salary packages.
  • Avoid the academic stress of attempting internal branch changes, which are heavily restricted or abolished in several IITs.
