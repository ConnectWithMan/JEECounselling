Overview of JEE Advanced Exam Pattern

The JEE Advanced exam is organized annually by one of the 7 zonal IITs under the guidance of the Joint Admission Board (JAB). It is the premier competitive entrance examination in India for admission into Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech), Integrated Master’s, and Dual Degree programs across all 23 Indian Institutes of Technology (IITs).

Top 2.5 lakh qualified candidates from JEE Main are eligible to register for JEE Advanced. The test consists of two mandatory computer-based papers: Paper 1 and Paper 2, conducted in two shifts on the same day.

Mandatory Requirement: Candidates must appear in BOTH Paper 1 and Paper 2. Candidates missing either Paper 1 or Paper 2 are marked absent and excluded from rank list generation.

Detailed Question Types & Partial Marking Schemes

Unlike JEE Main, the total marks and question distribution in JEE Advanced are not fixed beforehand. The exam features a diverse mix of question formats across Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics:

Single Choice MCQs

Questions with four options where exactly one option is correct.

+3 or +4 Full Marks -1 Negative Mark

Multi-Choice MCQs (Partial Marking)

Questions with four options where one or more options are correct.

+4 Full Marks +3 / +2 / +1 Partial Marks -2 Negative Mark

Numerical Value / Integer Questions

Questions requiring entry of a non-negative integer or decimal value using a virtual keyboard.

+3 or +4 Full Marks 0 No Negative Mark

Matching List / Matrix Match

Matching items from List-I with List-II option combinations.

+3 or +4 Full Marks -1 Negative Mark

Detailed Rules for Partial Marking

  • Full Marks (+4): Awarded only if all correct option(s) are selected and no incorrect option is chosen.
  • Partial Marks (+3): If all four options are correct, but candidate selects only three correct options.
  • Partial Marks (+2): If three or more options are correct, but candidate selects two correct options.
  • Partial Marks (+1): If two or more options are correct, but candidate selects only one correct option.
  • Negative Marks (-2): Applied if any incorrect option is selected, regardless of whether correct options were also marked.

Subject-Wise & Aggregate Qualifying Cutoff Criteria

To qualify for inclusion in the JEE Advanced Rank List (CRL and category-wise merit lists), candidates must clear two criteria: Minimum Subject-Wise Marks (in Physics, Chemistry, and Math individually) AND Minimum Aggregate Marks across both papers combined.

Architecture Aptitude Test (AAT) Exam Pattern

Candidates who qualify JEE Advanced and wish to secure admission into the 5-year B.Arch program offered by IIT Kharagpur, IIT Roorkee, and IIT BHU must appear for the Architecture Aptitude Test (AAT).

  • Test Mode: Offline pen-and-paper examination (3 hours duration).
  • Medium: English only.
  • Sections Covered: Freehand Drawing, Geometrical Drawing, Architectural Awareness, 3D Perception, and Imagination & Aesthetic Sensitivity.
  • Result & Grading: AAT has no numerical ranks; candidates are declared either 'Pass' or 'Fail' based on cut-off scores set by the Joint Implementation Committee.
