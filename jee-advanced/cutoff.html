What is JEE Advanced Cutoff 2027?

The term JEE Advanced cutoff 2027 refers to two distinct thresholds: (1) the qualifying cutoff — minimum marks in each subject and in aggregate required for a candidate to be included in the Common Rank List (CRL), and (2) the IIT admission cutoff — the opening and closing AIR ranks at which seats in specific IIT programs are allotted through JoSAA counselling.

Unlike JEE Main, JEE Advanced uses raw marks (not percentile) for qualifying. The total marks depend on the paper design for that year. Use our JEE Advanced College Predictor to estimate your IIT admission chances.

Factors Affecting JEE Advanced Cutoff 2027

Paper Difficulty & Total Marks

JEE Advanced paper structure changes yearly. When total marks are lower (e.g., 306 vs 360), absolute qualifying marks are also lower. The percentage criteria remain constant though.

Number of Qualifiers

Only ~2.5 lakh JEE Main candidates qualify for JEE Advanced. The actual number who appear is usually 1.5–1.8 lakh, making rank competition very tight at the top end of the rank list.

IIT Seat Matrix

JoSAA closing ranks depend on the number of available seats per category, program, and institute. New IIT programs or seat expansions can shift closing ranks significantly.

JoSAA Choice Filling & Rounds

Candidate choice patterns significantly influence closing ranks each year. Late withdrawals and seat vacancies across rounds can create surprising openings in premium programs.

How to Use JEE Advanced Cutoff to Plan IIT Admission

Strategic Steps

  • Check the official result to know your AIR and category rank.
  • Compare your AIR with JoSAA 2026 opening and closing ranks for target IITs.
  • Shortlist 15–20 IIT program combinations across reach, target, and safe categories.
  • Fill JoSAA choices in order of personal preference — rank first, branch second.
  • Don't lock a seat prematurely — use slide and float options across rounds.

Important Caveats

  • Don't compare your AIR with JEE Main CRL rank — these are completely different rank lists.
  • Don't forget Preparatory Course (PC) seats available for SC/ST/PwD at IITs.
  • Don't ignore Dual Degree (5-yr) programs — they often have lower closing AIR than B.Tech for the same branch.
  • Don't rely only on Round 1 — Round 6 final closing ranks are the most relevant benchmarks.
