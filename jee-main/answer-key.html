Provisional vs Final JEE Main Answer Key

Provisional Answer Key

  • Released immediately after the exam alongside candidate response sheets.
  • Contains draft answers formulated by NTA panel.
  • Open for challenges by students by paying ₹200 fee per question.
  • Subject to revisions, dropped questions, or multiple correct keys.

Final Answer Key

  • Published after senior expert committees evaluate all candidate challenges.
  • 100% binding and definitive — no further objections accepted.
  • Incorporates dropped questions or corrected option IDs.
  • Directly used by NTA's automated algorithms to compute your final percentile.

How to Download JEE Main Answer Key & Response Sheet 2027

Follow these verified steps to download your shift question paper, response sheet, and matching NTA answer key:

1

Navigate to NTA Official Portal

Visit jeemain.nta.nic.in or nta.ac.in on any secure browser.

2

Select Candidate Activity Link

Click on the designated notification link: "JEE(Main) 2027 Session 1 / Session 2 Answer Key Challenge & Recorded Response Sheet".

3

Authenticate with Login Credentials

Log in using your Application Number and Password (or Date of Birth) and enter the Captcha Security PIN.

4

Download Response Sheet & Master Question Paper

Click "View Question Paper / Response Sheet" to access your attempted responses alongside question IDs. Save it as a PDF for calculations.

5

Compare with Official Key Table

Open the Answer Key modal. Compare each Question ID with the official Correct Option ID to determine correct, incorrect, and unattempted answers.

Step-by-Step Procedure to Challenge JEE Main Answer Key

If you find a factual error, multiple correct answers, or an incorrect key formulated by NTA, you can submit an objection online during the 3-day challenge window:

1. Select Question ID

In your candidate portal, check the box next to the Question ID you wish to challenge in Section A or Section B.

2. Choose Claimed Option

Select the option ID you claim is correct. For numerical questions, enter your calculated value.

3. Upload Justification Proof

Upload standard reference book solutions or handwritten derivation proofs in a single consolidated PDF document.

4. Pay Processing Fee

Pay ₹200 per challenged question online via Debit Card, Credit Card, Net Banking, or UPI before deadline.

Expert Evaluation: If a challenge is accepted by the panel of subject experts, the key is revised across all shifts. Benefit is automatically extended to all eligible candidates, even if they didn't personally challenge the question.

Marking Scheme & Raw Score Calculation Formula

NTA implements negative marking across both Section A (Multiple Choice Questions) and Section B (Numerical Value Questions) in B.E. / B.Tech (Paper 1):

+4
Correct Response
-1
Incorrect Response (MCQ & NAT)
0
Unattempted / Marked for Review
JEE Main Raw Score Formula
Total Raw Score = (Total Correct × 4) - (Total Incorrect × 1)

Maximum possible marks = 300 (75 questions attempted out of 90 total questions; 25 per Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics).

Dropped Questions & Ambiguity Rules

Question Type Scenario Marking Resolution
Section A (MCQ) Question dropped / faulty options +4 Marks to ALL candidates who appeared in that shift.
Section A (MCQ) Two options found correct +4 Marks to candidates who marked either of the correct options.
Section B (Numerical) Question dropped / flawed data +4 Marks ONLY to candidates who attempted that question.
