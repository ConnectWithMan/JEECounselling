What is JEE Main Session 2 (April Attempt)?

JEE Main Session 2 is the second and final round of the National Testing Agency's (NTA) engineering entrance examination cycle, conducted annually in April. Session 2 provides aspirants a vital opportunity to improve their scores, rectify mistakes made in Session 1 (January), or appear as fresh candidates if they missed the earlier session.

Best of Two Policy

If you appear in both Jan and April, your highest NTA percentile is retained for AIR ranking.

JEE Advanced Gateway

Only the top 2,50,000 candidates post Session 2 qualify to sit for the prestigious IIT JEE Advanced.

NITs, IIITs & GFTIs

JoSAA & CSAB counselling allocations are determined entirely based on the final AIR released post Session 2.

Crucial Benefit: Over 70% of candidates experience a 5 to 15 percentile boost in Session 2 due to two extra months of revision, completed board exams, and familiarity with NTA computer-based exam environments.

How to Register for JEE Main Session 2

The application process for Session 2 depends on whether you registered during Session 1 (January) or are applying for the first time in the academic year:

Scenario A: Already Applied in Session 1

Fast 3-Step Process (No New Registration)

Candidates who registered for Session 1 do NOT need to fill the complete form again:

  • Step 1: Log in at jeemain.nta.nic.in using your existing Session 1 Application Number and Password.
  • Step 2: Select Course (Paper 1, 2A, or 2B), Question Paper Medium (English, Hindi, Regional), and 4 preferred Exam Cities for April.
  • Step 3: Pay the Session 2 Exam Fee online and download the Confirmation Page.

Scenario B: Fresh Candidate (New to 2027)

Complete Full Registration Process

Candidates who did not apply for Session 1 in January must complete the full registration:

  • Step 1: Authenticate via Aadhaar / DigiLocker / ABC ID to create login credentials.
  • Step 2: Enter personal details, communication address, Class 10 & 12 academic qualifications.
  • Step 3: Upload recent photograph (10-200 KB) and signature (4-30 KB) in JPEG format.
  • Step 4: Pay the application fee and save the Confirmation Page.

How NTA Calculates Best-of-Two Percentile & Final AIR

Because JEE Main is conducted across multiple shifts with differing difficulty levels, NTA does not use raw marks directly. Instead, scores are transformed into an NTA Percentile Score using statistical normalization.

The Golden Rule: If you appear in both Session 1 and Session 2, NTA automatically takes your HIGHEST total percentile score. A dip in Session 2 performance will NOT harm your Session 1 score!

Formula for NTA Percentile Score

Percentile = ( 100 × [No. of candidates with raw score ≤ Your raw score] ) ÷ [Total candidates in that shift]

Tie-Breaking Resolution Mechanism for All India Rank

If two or more candidates obtain identical total NTA Percentiles, NTA applies the following strict order of criteria to resolve ranks:

  • Criterion 1: Higher NTA Percentile Score in Mathematics.
  • Criterion 2: Higher NTA Percentile Score in Physics.
  • Criterion 3: Higher NTA Percentile Score in Chemistry.
  • Criterion 4: Lower proportion of attempted incorrect answers to correct answers across all subjects.
  • Criterion 5: Lower ratio of negative to positive responses specifically in Mathematics, followed by Physics and Chemistry.
  • Criterion 6: Candidate older in age.
  • Criterion 7: Ascending order of Application Number.
