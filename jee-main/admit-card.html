JEE Main 2027 Admit Card Release Schedule & Key Highlights

The JEE Main Admit Card is a mandatory document issued by the National Testing Agency (NTA). It permits registered candidates to enter the allocated exam center and sit for the Joint Entrance Examination (Main). The admit card contains critical information including candidate details, roll number, allotted test center name and address, exam date, shift timing, reporting hour, gate closing time, and mandatory test day rules. Candidates planning for the upcoming exam must check the complete JEE Main exam dates and ensure their application is completed per the official JEE Main application guide.

NTA Release Policy: In accordance with recent NTA security standards, admit cards are issued session-wise and phase-wise exactly 3 days prior to the candidate's designated exam date. Advance city intimation slips are published 7–10 days before the exam window starts.

How to Download NTA JEE Main Admit Card 2027

NTA provides two separate authentication methods for downloading the JEE Main hall ticket. Candidates can access their admit card either by using their password or by providing their date of birth alongside their application number.

1

Method A: Via Application Number & Password

  1. Visit the official NTA website: jeemain.nta.nic.in.
  2. Click on the active link labeled "JEE Main 2027 Session 1 / Session 2 Admit Card".
  3. Select the option: 'Through Application Number and Password'.
  4. Enter your JEE Main 2027 Application Number and Security Password.
  5. Type the security PIN displayed on screen and click 'Sign In'.
  6. Your hall ticket will display on screen. Download the PDF and take 2-3 clear color printouts.
2

Method B: Via Application Number & Date of Birth

  1. Open the official portal at jeemain.nta.nic.in.
  2. Locate and click the hall ticket download notification link.
  3. Choose the option: 'Through Application Number and Date of Birth'.
  4. Enter your JEE Main Application Number, Date of Birth (DD/MM/YYYY), and Security Captcha.
  5. Click on the 'Sign In' button.
  6. Verify all details on screen, hit the download button, and print the document on A4 sized paper.

Printout Recommendation: Always take a clear printout on standard white A4 paper. Ensure that your photograph, signature, barcode, and exam center details are crisp and legible. Do not print on both sides of the paper.

Essential Details to Verify on Your JEE Main Hall Ticket

After downloading your hall ticket, carefully review every line of text. Any mismatch between your admit card details and official identity proof may lead to disqualification at the test center. Verify your eligibility status beforehand using the JEE Main eligibility criteria guide.

Candidate Personal Details

Full Name, Father's Name, Mother's Name, Date of Birth, Gender, and Category (GEN/OBC-NCL/SC/ST/EWS).

Registration Credentials

JEE Main 2027 Application Form Number, Roll Number, State Code of Eligibility, and PwD Status.

Paper & Exam Schedule

Applied Paper (Paper 1 B.E./B.Tech or Paper 2 B.Arch/B.Planning), Exam Date, Shift Timing (Shift 1 / Shift 2), and Language Medium.

Test Center Allocation

Exact Test Center Name, Full Venue Address, Test Center Code, and Reporting/Gate Closing Time.

Visual Identifiers

Uploaded Passport Photo, Candidate Digital Signature, and Parent's Signature box.

Self-Declaration Undertaking

COVID/Health advisory compliance section requiring signature and thumb impression prior to arriving at venue.

Addressing Mismatches & Errors in JEE Main Admit Card

If you discover any error in your name, spelling mistakes, wrong category code, blurred photograph, signature mismatch, or incorrect exam center allocation, do not panic. Candidates are advised to immediately report the discrepancy to the NTA helpline.

NTA Rule on Errors: In cases where a candidate downloaded an admit card containing incorrect details, NTA permits the candidate to appear for the exam with the downloaded admit card while NTA administrative teams correct the record for subsequent result generation.

Exam Day Guidelines: Documents, Permissible Items & Dress Code

Strict adherence to NTA guidelines is enforced at all test centers across India and international centers. Candidates should review these lists prior to leaving for their assigned exam hall.

Documents & Items Allowed Inside the Exam Hall

  • Printed Admit Card: Clear A4 printout with filled Self-Declaration (Undertaking) form.
  • Original Authorized Photo ID: PAN Card, Driving License, Voter ID, Passport, Aadhaar Card (with photo), E-Aadhaar, or Ration Card. (Photocopies or digital IDs on phones will NOT be accepted).
  • Passport Photo: One additional passport-size photograph identical to the one uploaded during registration (to be pasted on attendance sheet).
  • Writing Instrument: One simple transparent ballpoint pen.
  • Personal Sanitizer: One small transparent bottle (50 ml).
  • Water Bottle: Transparent water bottle without any labels.
  • Special Note for B.Arch Candidates: Geometry box set, color pencils, erasers, and pencils (Water colors are prohibited).

Special Provisions for Diabetic Candidates

To support the health of diabetic students during the 3-hour computer-based test, NTA permits the following items inside the exam hall:

  • Fresh fruits such as apples, bananas, or oranges.
  • Sugar tablets / glucose tablets.
  • Transparent drinking water bottle.
  • Note: Packaged food items such as chips, chocolates, biscuits, or sandwiches are strictly prohibited.

Strictly Prohibited Items

  • Mobile phones, smartwatches, Bluetooth devices, earphones, microphones, pagers, or health bands.
  • Calculators, slide rules, log tables, electronic pens, or scanners.
  • Pencil boxes, geometry boxes (for B.Tech), purses, handbags, wallets, or paper sheets.
  • Eatables, tea, coffee, cold drinks, or packed snacks (except diabetic student provisions).

Official NTA Dress Code Rules

Dress Code Directives: Wear light, comfortable clothes with short sleeves. Avoid wearing clothes with large buttons, heavy embroidery, or metallic items. Wear simple sandals or slippers with thin soles (thick-soled shoes or boots are not permitted). Avoid wearing caps, mufflers, headgear, rings, bracelets, or metallic jewelry.

Difference Between Advance City Intimation Slip & Admit Card

Many candidates confuse the Advance City Intimation Slip with the actual Admit Card. Here is a clear breakdown of the differences:

Feature / Parameter Advance City Intimation Slip JEE Main Admit Card
Primary Purpose Informs candidate of the city allotted for exam to facilitate travel planning. Official hall ticket authorizing entry into the exact exam venue.
Release Timeline Issued 7 to 10 days before exam window starts. Issued 3 days prior to candidate's specific exam date.
Venue Information Contains City & State name only. Contains exact venue address, center code, shift time & gate closing hour.
Entry Requirement Cannot be used to enter the exam center hall. Mandatory document required for entry into exam hall.

How to Recover Forgotten Password or Application Number

If you forget your login password or application number while attempting to download the admit card, use the recovery tools on the official portal:

Steps to Recover Forgotten Password

  1. Visit jeemain.nta.nic.in and click on the admit card login link.
  2. Click on the 'Forgot Password?' link under the login box.
  3. Choose one of three reset options: Security Question, SMS OTP to registered mobile number, or Email reset link.
  4. Follow the prompts, enter the verification code received, and create a new password.

Steps to Recover Application Number

  1. Go to jeemain.nta.nic.in admit card portal.
  2. Click on the link 'Forgot Application Number?'.
  3. Enter Candidate Name, Father's Name, Mother's Name, Date of Birth, and State of Eligibility.
  4. Submit the security pin. Your Application Number will be sent via SMS and Email instantly.
