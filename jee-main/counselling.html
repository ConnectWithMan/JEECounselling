Overview of JEE Main Counselling 2027

Once the National Testing Agency (NTA) announces the JEE Main Session 2 result and All India Ranks (AIR), centralized counselling begins for seat allotment across premier engineering institutes in India. Qualified candidates participate in JoSAA Counselling for standard rounds and subsequently in CSAB Special Rounds for remaining vacant seats.

In addition to the central NIT+ system, over 500+ private and state government universities across Delhi (JAC Delhi), Rajasthan (REAP), Maharashtra (MHT CET counselling), Haryana (HSTES), and Uttar Pradesh (UPTAC) conduct state-level counselling utilizing JEE Main Paper 1 scores.

Key Takeaway: Only one common registration on JoSAA is required to apply for all 31 NITs, 25 IIITs, 33 GFTIs, and 23 IITs (for JEE Advanced qualifiers). You do NOT need to apply to each NIT or IIIT individually.

Eligibility Criteria for JEE Main Counselling

To participate in JoSAA and CSAB counselling for admissions into NITs, IIITs, and GFTIs, candidates must satisfy both academic and rank requirements:

Class 12 Board Percentage Criteria

  • General, EWS & OBC-NCL: Minimum 75% aggregate marks in Class 12 or equivalent qualifying examination.
  • SC, ST & PwD: Minimum 65% aggregate marks in Class 12.
  • Top 20 Percentile Alternative: Candidates who feature in the top 20 percentile category of their state board are also fully eligible.
  • Mandatory Subjects: Physics and Mathematics along with Chemistry/Biology/Biotechnology/Technical Vocational subject.

Rank & Category Criteria

  • Must hold a valid non-zero score and rank in JEE Main 2027 Paper 1 (B.Tech) or Paper 2 (B.Arch/B.Plan).
  • Category certificates (OBC-NCL, GEN-EWS) must be issued on or after April 1, 2027 in the prescribed central format.
  • State of Eligibility is determined by where the candidate passed Class 12 (not native domicile place).
  • No upper age restriction applies for participation in JoSAA counselling.

Important Note on State of Eligibility: If you studied Class 12 in Kota (Rajasthan) but live in Uttar Pradesh, your Home State quota is Rajasthan, NOT Uttar Pradesh. Home State eligibility is strictly tied to the location of the Class 12 school/examination center.

Step-by-Step JEE Main Counselling Procedure (JoSAA)

JoSAA follows a completely computerized, transparent seat allotment procedure. Follow these exact sequential steps to ensure error-free participation:

1

Online Registration & Verification

Visit the official portal at josaa.nic.in. Log in using your JEE Main 2027 Application Number and password. Verify personal details, nationality, state code of eligibility, and gender. Confirm contact details and mobile number for OTP alerts.

2

Choice Filling of Institutes & Branches

Select your desired engineering branches across available NITs, IIITs, and GFTIs. There is no restriction on the number of choices you can fill. Reorder choices using 'Move Up', 'Move Down', or 'Swap' tools. Always place top-tier institutes and preferred branches at the top.

3

Mock Seat Allotments (1 & 2)

JoSAA releases two mock seat allocations before locking. These trial allocations simulate your probable allotment based on choices submitted by other aspirants up to that point. Use mock results to analyze your realistic admission chances and reorder preferences.

4

Choice Locking

Once you are completely satisfied with your preference order, lock your choices using your account password. Take a printout of the locked choice list. If you forget to lock your choices before the deadline, your last saved choices lock automatically.

5

Seat Allocation Result

JoSAA runs its allotment algorithm based on Merit (CRL/Category Rank), preference list, seat matrix, and category reservation. Check the result of Round 1 on the candidate portal and download the provisional seat allotment letter.

6

Selecting Willingness: Freeze, Float, or Slide

If allotted a seat, you MUST submit your willingness choice online. Choose Freeze (accept and lock the seat), Float (accept but seek higher preferences in any institute in next rounds), or Slide (accept but seek higher branch within the same institute).

7

Payment of Seat Acceptance Fee (SAF) & Document Upload

Pay the mandatory Seat Acceptance Fee online (₹35,000 for GEN/GEN-EWS/OBC-NCL, ₹17,500 for SC/ST/PwD). Upload scanned copies of required original documents (10th, 12th marksheet, category certificate, medical certificate, cancelled cheque).

8

Online Document Verification & Query Response

The reporting center verifies uploaded documents virtually. If any clarification or missing document is flagged, a query appears in your login. You must submit the requested document within the stipulated deadline, or the allotted seat will be forfeited.

9

Partial Admission Fee (PAF) & Final Physical Reporting

After the conclusion of JoSAA Round 5/6, candidates retaining a seat in an NIT, IIIT, or GFTI must pay the Partial Admission Fee (PAF) on the JoSAA portal to confirm admission. Subsequently, complete physical reporting at the allotted campus with original documents.

Understanding Freeze, Float, and Slide Options

Once an applicant is allotted a seat in any round, selecting the correct willingness option is crucial. Here is how each option works and when you should use it:

Freeze

You accept the allotted seat permanently. You are 100% satisfied with the institute and branch. You will not be considered for any higher choices in subsequent rounds.

Best when: You got your top-priority dream branch & college.
Float

You accept the current seat as a confirmed backup, but remain open to being upgraded to any higher preference choice in any institute in upcoming rounds.

Best when: You want a better branch or better institute in next rounds.
Slide

You accept the current seat, but you want to upgrade only to a higher academic program within the same allotted institute in subsequent rounds.

Best when: You love the college (e.g. NIT Trichy) and only want a branch upgrade there.

Documents Checklist for JEE Main Counselling

Ensure you have digital scanned copies (PDF or JPEG format, 10 KB to 500 KB) ready before choice locking starts. Keep at least 4 sets of physical copies for campus reporting:

Academic & Identity Documents

  • Class 10 Marksheet & Certificate (as proof of Date of Birth).
  • Class 12 Marksheet & Passing Certificate.
  • JEE Main 2027 Admit Card & Official Scorecard.
  • Provisional Seat Allotment Letter (downloaded from JoSAA/CSAB portal).
  • Valid Govt Photo ID Proof (Aadhaar Card, Passport, or Voter ID).
  • Proof of Seat Acceptance Fee Payment receipt.

Category & Special Certificates

  • OBC-NCL Certificate: Issued on or after April 1, 2027, as per Central Government format.
  • GEN-EWS Certificate: Issued on or after April 1, 2027, in Government of India format.
  • SC / ST Certificate: Original caste certificate issued by competent authority.
  • Medical Fitness Certificate: As per JoSAA official Format (signed by registered medical practitioner).
  • PwD Certificate: Issued by authorized Medical Board with UDID card if available.
  • Cancelled Cheque / Bank Passbook for potential fee refunds.

CSAB 2027 Special Rounds Guide

After the conclusion of all JoSAA rounds, seats that remain unoccupied due to seat withdrawals, non-reporting, or cancellations are pooled into the CSAB Special Rounds (Round 1 & Round 2).

Who Can Participate in CSAB?

  • Candidates who did not register in JoSAA.
  • Candidates registered in JoSAA who were not allotted any seat.
  • Candidates who were allotted a seat in JoSAA but withdrew or had it cancelled.
  • Candidates who secured an NIT/IIIT/GFTI seat in JoSAA and wish to upgrade.

Key CSAB Rules to Remember

  • Fresh registration and separate participation fee are mandatory for CSAB.
  • You must fill fresh choices. JoSAA choices are NOT carried forward.
  • If allotted a new seat in CSAB, your previous JoSAA seat is automatically lost.
  • IITs do NOT participate in CSAB Special Rounds. Only NITs, IIITs, and GFTIs take part.

Smart Choice-Filling Strategy & Mistakes to Avoid

Choice filling determines your seat outcome far more than students realize. The JoSAA computer algorithm scans your choices strictly from #1 downwards until it finds an open seat matching your rank:

Best Practices to Follow

  • Bucket into Dream, Realistic & Safe: Fill ~25% dream choices (ranks higher than yours), ~50% realistic choices (near your rank), and ~25% safe backup choices.
  • Fill Maximum Choices: There is no fee per choice. Filling 150–250 choices prevents missing out on late-round vacancies.
  • Strict True Preference Order: Never put a college you dislike higher simply because its previous cutoff was high.
  • Analyze Home State vs Other State: Leverage your Home State quota in your state's NIT for branches that close higher.

Critical Pitfalls to Avoid

  • Filling Limited Choices: Candidates who fill only 10–15 choices risk getting zero seat allotments across all rounds.
  • Putting Lower Preferred Seats Higher: Remember that once allotted, you can NEVER float down to lower numbered choices.
  • Missing Verification Queries: Failing to answer an online document query within 24 hours leads to instantaneous seat forfeiture.
  • Forgetting to Pay Partial Admission Fee: Without PAF, your allotted JoSAA seat is cancelled before campus reporting.

JoSAA (Joint Seat Allocation Authority) conducts the main centralized admission rounds (5–6 rounds) for IITs, NITs, IIITs, and GFTIs. CSAB (Central Seat Allocation Board) conducts two special spot rounds after all JoSAA rounds are finished, specifically to fill leftover vacant seats in NITs, IIITs, and GFTIs. CSAB does not handle admissions for IITs.
To secure admission to NITs, IIITs, and GFTIs, General/OBC-NCL/EWS candidates must score at least 75% aggregate marks in Class 12 (65% for SC/ST/PwD) OR rank within the top 20 percentile of their respective Class 12 educational board.
