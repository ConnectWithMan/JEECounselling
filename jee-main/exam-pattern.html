Overview of NTA JEE Main 2027 Exam Pattern

The National Testing Agency (NTA) conducts the JEE Main 2027 exam in two sessions (Session 1 in January and Session 2 in April). JEE Main serves as the entrance test for admission to B.E./B.Tech, B.Arch, and B.Planning programs across NITs, IIITs, CFTIs, and participating State Engineering Colleges, as well as the qualifying exam for JEE Advanced.

The examination is categorized into three distinct papers based on the program chosen by candidates:

  • Paper 1 (B.E. / B.Tech): Conducted entirely online in Computer-Based Test (CBT) mode across Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics.
  • Paper 2A (B.Arch): Conducted in a hybrid mode. Mathematics and Aptitude sections are online (CBT), while the Drawing section is conducted offline on an A4 sheet.
  • Paper 2B (B.Planning): Conducted entirely online (CBT) covering Mathematics, Aptitude Test, and Planning-based questions.

NTA Key Directive: Candidates have the flexibility to apply for Session 1, Session 2, or both sessions. If a candidate appears for both sessions, NTA considers their best score among the two sessions for final rank compilation.

JEE Main Paper 1 (B.E. / B.Tech) Exam Pattern

Paper 1 is designed for students seeking undergraduate engineering admissions (B.E./B.Tech). Each of the three core subjects (Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics) is split into two distinct sections: Section A and Section B.

Key Rules for Section A & Section B

  • Section A (MCQs): Contains multiple-choice questions with 4 options and 1 correct answer. All 20 questions in each subject are compulsory.
  • Section B (Numerical Value Questions): Candidates enter a numerical value as their answer using an on-screen virtual keyboard. Out of 10 options per subject, candidates must select and answer exactly 5 questions.
  • If a candidate attempts more than 5 questions in Section B, only the first 5 attempted questions will be evaluated by the NTA automated system.

Official NTA Marking Scheme & Negative Marking Rules

NTA follows a uniform evaluation protocol for objective and numerical value questions across Paper 1, Paper 2A, and Paper 2B:

+4
Correct Response
Full marks awarded for selecting the exact correct option or numerical value.
-1
Incorrect Response
1 mark deducted for wrong answers in both Section A (MCQs) and Section B (NVQs).
0
Unattempted / Dropped
No marks added or deducted for unattempted questions or marked-for-review questions.

Important Note on Dropped Questions: If a question is found to be incorrect or dropped by NTA after the provisional answer key challenge window, +4 marks are awarded to all candidates who attempted the question, regardless of their selected option (or to all candidates who appeared if it is a Section A question).

Smart Exam Pattern Strategies & Preparation Tips

Understanding the exam pattern enables candidates to optimize their time management and maximize their raw score on test day:

Section B Selection Strategy

Spend the initial 2-3 minutes scanning all 10 Numerical Value Questions per subject. Pick the 5 simplest and most direct calculations to avoid unnecessary negative marks.

Time Allocation Per Subject

Aim for 45-50 minutes on Chemistry, 60 minutes on Physics, and 70 minutes on Mathematics to handle lengthy algebraic and calculus problems efficiently.

Master Virtual Keyboard

Practice entering exact rounded-off integer or decimal values into virtual keyboards during mock tests to prevent formatting errors during official CBT sessions.
