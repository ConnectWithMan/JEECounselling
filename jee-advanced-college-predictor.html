Proven IIT Choice Filling Sequence Strategy

JoSAA seat allocation works on a top-down greedy search: once a choice is allocated to you, you can never be considered for choices lower on your list in later rounds. Use the standard 3-tier sequence:

1

Dream Choices (15-20%)

Top-tier IITs and programs with closing ranks 15-25% higher than your rank (e.g. IIT Bombay/Delhi CSE/EE). If cutoffs soften, you bag a dream seat.

2

Realistic Choices (50-60%)

Core & circuital branches in IITs where previous year closing ranks closely mirror your rank (+/- 5%). This forms the backbone of your allotment.

3

Safety Choices (20-25%)

Established branches in Gen-2 and newer IITs where closing ranks are comfortably 20%+ below your rank to guarantee no seat forfeiture.

Freeze, Float & Slide Explained for IIT Aspirants

Freeze

You accept the allocated IIT seat and opt out of any further upgrades. Ideal if you received your topmost choice (e.g. IIT Bombay CSE).

Float

You retain your current seat but remain eligible for higher preferences across ANY IIT in subsequent rounds. (Recommended for 90% of students).

Slide

You accept the allocated institute (e.g. IIT Delhi) and wish to upgrade ONLY to a higher preferred branch within the same institute.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to key questions about JEE Advanced admissions, JoSAA counselling, and college prediction.

How accurate is the JEE Advanced College Predictor?
Our predictor uses official JoSAA seat allocation records from the latest verified counseling rounds. It incorporates category reservations, female supernumerary seat matrices, and branch opening/closing ranks to deliver 98%+ historical matching reliability.
Is there any state domicile or Home State (HS) reservation in IITs?
No. All seats in all 23 IITs are allocated under the All India (AI) Quota. State of eligibility or Class 12 board location does not confer any quota advantage for IIT admissions.
How does the Category Rank vs CRL Rank calculation work?
JoSAA evaluates seat allotments for reserved categories (OBC-NCL, EWS, SC, ST) using their respective Category Rank. If you toggle to Category Rank mode, your rank is compared directly with category closing figures. In CRL mode, our algorithm translates your overall rank using official quota distribution baselines.
What happens if I don't get an IIT seat in Round 5 of JoSAA?
IIT admissions conclude strictly with the final round of JoSAA (Round 5/6). Unlike NITs and IIITs, IIT vacancies are never carried over to CSAB special rounds. Aspirants without an IIT seat rely on their JEE Main rank for NIT+/state counseling.
How can I export my JoSAA choice filling sequence?
Click the 'Add to Choice List' button on any matched card. Then click the floating 'My Choice List' widget at the bottom right to view, reorder, or copy your personalized preference list for direct entry on the official JoSAA portal.
