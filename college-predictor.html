JoSAA 2026 Counselling Process

1
Registration
Register at josaa.nic.in using JEE credentials. Pay registration fee and fill personal details.
2
Choice Filling
Fill up to 75+ institute-branch choices in order of preference. Lock before deadline.
3
Seat Allotment (6 Rounds)
JoSAA runs 6 rounds. Use Freeze, Float, or Slide to manage upgrades across rounds.
4
Seat Acceptance Fee
Pay fee (Rs 35,000 IITs / Rs 25,000 NITs) to secure your allotted seat after each round.
5
Document Verification
Upload Class 10/12 certificates, scorecard, category certificate, and medical certificate online.
6
Report to Institute
Report physically to your allotted institute with original documents during the reporting window.
