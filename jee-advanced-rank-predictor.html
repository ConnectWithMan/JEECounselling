Official Joint Admission Board (JAB) Qualifying Criteria

To be included in the rank list of JEE Advanced, a candidate must satisfy both the minimum subject-wise percentage in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics AND the minimum aggregate percentage:

Official JEE Advanced Tie-Breaking Protocol

If two or more candidates obtain identical aggregate scores in JEE Advanced, the following tie-breaking sequence is invoked to determine rank priority:

  1. Higher Positive Marks: The candidate with higher positive marks across both Paper 1 and Paper 2 will be given a higher rank.
  2. Higher Marks in Mathematics: If positive marks are equal, the candidate with higher total marks in Mathematics receives the higher rank.
  3. Higher Marks in Physics: If Mathematics marks are also equal, Physics marks are evaluated.
  4. Identical Rank Allotment: If all the above parameters remain equal, the candidates are awarded the same rank (shared rank).
