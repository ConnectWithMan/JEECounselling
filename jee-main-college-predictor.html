Home State vs Other State Quota Rules

One of the biggest advantages in NIT admissions is the 50% Home State reservation. Here is how JoSAA calculates eligibility:

1

Class 12th Location

Your eligibility for Home State quota is 100% defined by the state from where you completed or appeared for Class 12 / Intermediate board exams.

2

No Permanent Address Clash

If you live in Bihar but completed schooling in Delhi, you are strictly eligible for NIT Delhi under Home State quota, and considered Other State for NIT Patna.

3

Dual Pool Processing

JoSAA automatically evaluates home state candidates in both the All India / Other State pool and the Home State pool, awarding the best possible choice.

Understanding CSAB Special Rounds

After Round 5 of JoSAA completes, remaining vacant seats in NITs, IIITs, and GFTIs are transferred to the Central Seat Allocation Board (CSAB) for two special rounds. Cutoffs often drop drastically during CSAB (sometimes by 10,000 to 40,000 ranks in core & circuital branches). If your rank is outside JoSAA closing boundaries, CSAB is your prime opportunity to secure an NIT or IIIT seat.

Frequently Asked Questions

Verified information regarding JEE Main cutoff analysis, JoSAA seat allotment, and college prediction.

How accurate is the JEE Main College Predictor?
The predictor is built using official JoSAA Round 5/6 and CSAB final seat allocation cutoffs. It includes state-specific home quotas, female supernumerary seat reservations, and category cutoffs for all 31 NITs, 26 IIITs, and 33 GFTIs.
Can a student with rank 50,000+ get an NIT?
Yes! For General category students with rank 50,000 - 80,000, core branches (Civil, Metallurgy, Chemical, Mining) in mid-tier and newer NITs, or through Home State quota in North-Eastern NITs/NIT Srinagar, are highly feasible, especially in CSAB rounds.
What is the difference between IIITs and NITs?
NITs offer a full spectrum of engineering disciplines (CSE, ECE, EE, Mech, Civil, Chem, Metallurgy), whereas IIITs (Indian Institutes of Information Technology) specialize almost exclusively in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, and Electronics. IIITs generally do not have Home State quota.
How do I export my choice filling list?
Click 'Add to Choices' on any college card. Open 'My JoSAA Choice List' using the bottom-right floating trigger to review, reorder, or copy your personalized choice filling order directly into the JoSAA choice registration portal.
