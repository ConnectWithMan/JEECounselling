What is CSAB Counselling 2027?

The Central Seat Allocation Board (CSAB) conducts special counselling rounds to fill all residual vacant seats across 32 NITs, 26 IIITs, IIEST Shibpur, and 40 GFTIs after the completion of all JoSAA regular rounds and reporting.

Thousands of premier engineering seats remain unoccupied each year due to seat cancellations, non-payment of partial admission fees (PAF), or students opting for IITs/BITS. CSAB Special Rounds offer an incredible opportunity for candidates to secure significant branch and college upgrades at much higher closing ranks.

Key Insight: IITs do not take part in CSAB Special Rounds. CSAB conducts admissions exclusively for the NIT+ System using candidates' JEE Main 2027 CRL (Common Rank List) Ranks. Category ranks are used only to determine eligibility, but allocation order follows CRL rank.

CSAB 2027 Fee Structure, Participation Fee & Refund Rules

CSAB requires a Special Round Participation Fee (SRPF) at the time of registration. If you already paid JoSAA Seat Acceptance Fee (SAF) and Partial Admission Fee (PAF), your fees are automatically adjusted as detailed below:

Candidate Category / JoSAA Status GEN / GEN-EWS / OBC-NCL SC / ST / PwD Fee Breakdown & Adjustment
Fresh Candidates / No JoSAA Allotment ₹44,000 ₹24,000 ₹5,000 Non-refundable Processing + ₹39k/₹19k Institute Fee
Candidates with JoSAA Seat (SAF + PAF Paid) ₹5,000 ₹5,000 Only non-refundable CSAB processing fee payable
Candidates who paid JoSAA SAF but Withdrew/No PAF ₹5,000 or Differential ₹5,000 or Differential Adjusted against refundable balance of JoSAA SAF

Refund If No Seat Is Allotted

If a candidate is not allotted any seat in either CSAB Special Round 1 or Round 2, the participation fee is refunded after deducting only the ₹5,000 processing charge.

Refund If Seat Allotted & Surrendered

If allotted a fresh seat in Special Round 1 and you choose Surrender and Exit within the deadline, your fee is refunded after deducting the ₹7,000 withdrawal processing fee.

Seat Allocation Decisions & Willingness Options in CSAB

Upon receiving an allotment in CSAB Special Round 1, candidates have 5 distinct willingness options:

Willingness Option Meaning & Implication Impact on Existing / JoSAA Seat
Freeze Accept the allotted seat finally. You do not wish to participate in Special Round 2. Seat confirmed. You proceed directly to institute reporting.
Float Accept current seat, but remain open for higher choices across all colleges in Round 2. Current seat safely retained if higher choice is not allotted.
Slide Accept current seat, but want higher branches within the same allotted institute in Round 2. Current branch retained if higher branch not allotted.
Surrender & Participate Reject currently allotted seat, but participate in Round 2 for other higher choices. Allotted seat is released to the vacancy pool; no claim on it.
Surrender & Exit Reject currently allotted seat and exit CSAB completely. Candidate exits CSAB. Eligible for refund as per rules.
