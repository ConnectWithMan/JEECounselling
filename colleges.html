Overview of JoSAA & CSAB Participating Colleges

The Joint Seat Allocation Authority (JoSAA) conducts unified online counselling for seat allocation across India's premier technical institutions: the 23 Indian Institutes of Technology (IITs), 31 National Institutes of Technology (NITs), 26 Indian Institutes of Information Technology (IIITs), and 38+ Government Funded Technical Institutions (GFTIs).

Admissions to IITs require candidates to qualify JEE Main and subsequently crack the JEE Advanced examination. Admissions to all NITs, IIITs, and GFTIs are administered strictly on the basis of candidates' All India Ranks (CRL & Category Ranks) in JEE Main Paper 1 (B.E./B.Tech) or Paper 2 (B.Arch/B.Planning).

National Institutes of Technology (31 NITs)

The 31 National Institutes of Technology (NITs) are premier centrally funded engineering institutions located in every major Indian state. Seat allocation in NITs follows a distinct dual-quota policy:

  • 50% Home State (HS) Quota: Exclusively reserved for candidates whose Class 12 board examination examination center was in the institute's home state.
  • 50% Other State (OS) Quota: Open to candidates across all Indian states and Union Territories based on JEE Main All India Ranks (CRL).
  • Supernumerary Seats: Special female supernumerary quota to ensure at least 20% female representation across each branch.

Indian Institutes of Information Technology (26 IIITs)

The 26 IIITs are specialized academies focused on cutting-edge computer science, software engineering, artificial intelligence, and electronics. They fall under two distinct governance models:

5 MHRD/MoE-Funded IIITs

100% centrally funded government institutions offering subsidized fees, extensive central research grants, and high academic prestige:

  • IIIT Allahabad (Jhalwa, UP)
  • ABV-IIITM Gwalior (MP)
  • PDPM IIITDM Jabalpur (MP)
  • IIITDM Kancheepuram (Chennai, TN)
  • IIITDM Kurnool (AP)

21 Public-Private Partnership (PPP) IIITs

Co-founded by Central Gov (50%), State Gov (35%), and industry partners (15%) like TCS, Infosys, and Tech Mahindra. High placement focus in tech domains:

  • IIIT Lucknow, IIIT Pune, IIIT Kota
  • IIIT Sri City, IIIT Vadodara, IIIT Guwahati
  • IIIT Nagpur, IIIT Surat, IIIT Bhopal
  • IIIT Kalyani, IIIT Sonepat, IIIT Dharwad

All India Quota (AI): Unlike NITs, almost all IIITs allocate seats purely on an All-India Quota (AI) basis without state domicile restrictions, making them ideal targets for students with strong JEE Main ranks regardless of home state.

Female Supernumerary Quota

To improve gender diversity in technical engineering fields, supernumerary seats are created in all IITs and NITs to ensure a minimum of 20% female enrollment in every course.

These supernumerary seats do not reduce the number of open seats available to male aspirants; they are created as additional allocations under JoSAA Seat Allotment rules.

Admission Pathways & Eligibility Guidelines

IIT Admission Pathway

  1. Appear in JEE Main 2026 (Session 1 and/or Session 2).
  2. Qualify among the top 2,50,000 candidates as per the official JEE Main Cutoff.
  3. Register and appear in JEE Advanced 2026 after checking the syllabus and pattern.
  4. Achieve minimum qualifying percentage marks in each subject and aggregate.
  5. Participate in JoSAA 2026 online choice filling.

NIT, IIIT & GFTI Pathway

  1. Appear in JEE Main 2026 Paper 1 after reviewing the syllabus.
  2. Obtain an All India Rank (CRL) based on your best NTA Percentile.
  3. Fulfill the 75% Class 12th criteria (65% for SC/ST/PwD) or Top 20 percentile.
  4. Register on JoSAA and submit preferences across NITs/IIITs/GFTIs.
  5. If seats remain vacant, participate in CSAB Special Rounds.

Board Performance Rule: Candidates must have passed Class 12 with Physics, Mathematics, and one of Chemistry/Biotechnology/Biology/Technical Vocational subject. Read the full JEE Eligibility Criteria for exemptions and state board rules.

Frequently Asked Questions (Colleges & Admissions)

A total of 118+ institutes participate in JoSAA counselling: 23 Indian Institutes of Technology (IITs), 31 National Institutes of Technology (NITs), 26 Indian Institutes of Information Technology (IIITs), and 38+ Government Funded Technical Institutions (GFTIs).

In all 31 NITs, 50% of the total seats are reserved under the Home State (HS) quota for students who passed their Class 12 board examination in that state. The other 50% of seats are allocated under the Other State (OS) quota for students across the rest of India.
5 IIITs (Allahabad, Gwalior, Jabalpur, Kancheepuram, Kurnool) are 100% centrally funded government institutions. The remaining 21 IIITs operate on a Public-Private Partnership (PPP) model, founded jointly by the Ministry of Education, respective state governments, and industry sponsors. All are recognized as Institutes of National Importance.
To secure admission in any IIT, NIT, IIIT, or GFTI through JoSAA, general category candidates must have secured at least 75% aggregate in their Class 12 board exams, or rank in the top 20 percentile of their respective board. For SC, ST, and PwD candidates, the minimum aggregate is 65%.
JoSAA conducts 6 rounds of unified counselling for all IITs, NITs, IIITs, and GFTIs. After JoSAA concludes, CSAB conducts 2 Special Rounds exclusively for the vacant seats remaining in NITs, IIITs, and GFTIs. IIT seats are not filled via CSAB Special Rounds.
To improve gender diversity in technical engineering fields, extra supernumerary seats are created across IITs and NITs ensuring at least a 20% female student enrollment target per branch without reducing the existing open/gender-neutral seat pool.
IITs do not have any state domicile quotas; 100% of seats are allocated purely on an All India (AI) basis. Similarly, almost all IIITs allocate seats purely on an All India basis without state restrictions, giving equal opportunity to candidates from all regions across India.
