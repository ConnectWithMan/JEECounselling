JEE Main 2026 Information Bulletin & Registration Schedule Announced

The National Testing Agency (NTA) has outlined the operational schedule for Joint Entrance Examination (Main) 2026. Online application for Session 1 starts in late November, with the examination scheduled across two shifts in late January 2026.

  • Aadhaar / DigiLocker Verification: Seamless identity syncing initiated at registration stage to prevent impersonation.
  • Two Session System: Candidates can choose Session 1 (January), Session 2 (April), or both; best NTA Score used for ranking.
  • Paper 1 Structure: 75 questions total (25 Physics, 25 Chemistry, 25 Maths) with standard +4 / -1 negative marking across all sections.
