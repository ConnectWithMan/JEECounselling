What is DASA UG Scheme 2027?

Direct Admission of Students Abroad (DASA) is a flagship Government of India scheme coordinated under the Ministry of Education. It facilitates direct admission for Foreign Nationals, Non-Resident Indians (NRIs), Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs), and Overseas Citizens of India (OCIs) to premier Centrally Funded Technical Institutions, including NITs, IIEST Shibpur, IIITs, and Schools of Planning and Architecture (SPAs).

Admissions under DASA are supernumerary (over and above regular sanctioned capacity) up to 15% of total seats. All admissions are conducted strictly based on the candidate's JEE Main 2027 CRL (Common Rank List) rank conducted by the National Testing Agency.

DASA Scheme vs CIWG (Gulf Quota) Scheme Comparison

CIWG (Children of Indian Workers in Gulf) is a distinct sub-category within DASA where 1/3rd (5% out of 15%) of total supernumerary seats are reserved exclusively for children of Indian parents employed in the 6 GCC Gulf nations.

DASA & CIWG 2027 Detailed Eligibility Criteria

Academic Qualifications

  • Passed Class 12 / High School Board with Mathematics & Physics as compulsory subjects along with Chemistry/Bio/CS.
  • Minimum 75% aggregate marks (or CGPA of 7.5 out of 10) in Class 12.
  • Valid JEE Main 2027 CRL Rank from Session 1 or Session 2.

Residential & Age Criteria

  • Candidates must have completed at least 2 years of education abroad (Class 11 and 12) during the last 8 years.
  • Must hold a valid Foreign Passport or Indian Passport with valid residency/student visa.
  • Date of Birth should be on or after October 1, 2002.

Proof of 2 Years Education Abroad: Candidates must upload study certificates stamped by the school principal certifying that Class 11 and Class 12 were completed from an institution located outside India. Indian schools located abroad (CBSE schools in GCC/UAE) are fully eligible.

Mandatory Document Checklist for DASA & CIWG

  • Passport Copy: Valid Passport of the candidate showing nationality or foreign residency visa.
  • Class 10 & Class 12 Certificates: Marksheets and passing certificates from accredited board/school.
  • Proof of 2 Years Education Abroad: School completion/attendance certificate covering 11th and 12th standard.
  • JEE Main 2027 Scorecard: Official NTA Scorecard with CRL Rank.
  • CIWG Specific Proofs (for Gulf Quota only): Valid work permit / resident visa of parent, parent's passport, and employer certificate on company letterhead showing current employment in GCC nation.
